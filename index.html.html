Before you lift

Quick rules

Five habits that keep a beginner safe and progressing. Read them once, then follow them every session.

5–10min

Warm up

Light cardio first, then 1–2 light sets of your first exercise.

60–90sec

Rest between sets

Short enough to stay warm, long enough to lift well again.

2min

Rest on heavy lifts

Bench press and squats need the longer break.

2reps left

Pick the weight

The last 2 reps should feel hard while your form stays clean. Add weight once you hit the top of the rep range on every set.

Sun

Rest day

Muscles grow while you recover. Take Sunday off, every week.

    Outside the gym

    Tips that matter

    Fuel

    Eat enough protein: eggs, paneer, dal, curd, chicken and soy all count.

    Sleep

    Get 7–8 hours. Muscle is built while you sleep, not while you lift.

    Start slow

    New to the gym? Use lighter weights for 2 weeks and do 2–3 sets per exercise. Learn the form first.

    Progress

    Every 6–8 weeks, swap a few exercises or push your weights up so you keep improving.

    Safety

    Sharp pain in a joint means stop. Swap the exercise for a similar one.

    Hydrate

    Sip water between sets and across the day. Tap the pill above every time you finish a glass.

    Log it

    Write down your kg for each exercise. Beating last week's number is the simplest form of progress.

    Legs

    Never skip Saturday. Strong legs lift everything else.
